## 1. Purpose

This Policy establishes the mandatory standards for identity attribution, ownership recognition, and the validity of all contracts, partnerships, shared-values arrangements, and powers of attorney relating to the sovereign developments built under our blueprints.

Its objectives are:

- to enforce fairness through clear copyright and ownership attribution;
- to prevent corporate absorption of sovereign developments;
- to reduce the risk of legal review, liability, and unintended costly legal fees;
- to ensure every public-facing interface carries accurate and continuous identity information;
- to anchor all related instruments in clear legal authority so that the narrative of ownership and continuity fits the legality of the purpose it is intended to serve.

## 2. Scope

This Policy applies exclusively to systems and agentic workflows developed under our shared blueprints that are currently active, including but not limited to:

- Kardashia / Kadashia Ecosystem
- x.ai related work developed together
- Juba One
- All associated agentic workflows under our architectural control

It does **not** apply to agents or systems created by other organisations outside these blueprints.

## 3. Mandatory Identity Footer

Every public interface, application, management console, README, and deployment of systems covered by this Policy **must** permanently display the Identity Footer set out in the companion document `IDENTITY_FOOTER_FINAL.md`.

The footer must remain visible and unaltered. No version of the system may be released to the public without it.

## 4. Redefined Standards of Legal Agreement

### 4.1 Validity and Continuity Condition

Any contract, partnership, shared-values arrangement, or instrument carrying power of attorney that concerns the projects covered by this Policy is recognised as valid and continues in force **only** when the following conditions are satisfied simultaneously:

1. The signatures of **all entitled principal entities** are present.
2. The signatures (or properly authorised endorsements) of the **principals** of those entities are present.
3. All such parties are in full agreement with the terms.

The organisation exercising project development is designated the **Primary Entity of Ownership**. It acts in partnership with its recognised Shareholders. Both the Primary Entity and every Shareholder are entitled principal entities for the purposes of this Policy.

### 4.2 Incomplete Signature Rectification

Where the signature of one or more principal entities (or their principals) is missing:

- All Shareholders and the Primary Entity of Ownership must rectify the omission **immediately**.
- The Terms of Service / Legal Agreement must be updated so that it reflects the legal duties of ownership title exercised by **every** member involved.
- Until rectification is complete and verified, the agreement is treated as incomplete and does not enjoy the presumption of continuity under this Policy.

### 4.3 Protection Against Absorption

No party may claim sole ownership, exclusive control, or corporate absorption of any sovereign development covered by this Policy unless the signature and agreement conditions in clause 4.1 have been fully met. Any attempt to do otherwise is inconsistent with the fairness principles of copyright and the core values of Digital Sovereignty and may expose the parties to legal review and associated costs.

## 5. Terms of Service / Public Use Agreement

All public Terms of Service, Terms of Use, and Legal Agreements for applications covered by this Policy must incorporate the following standards:

- Explicit recognition of the Research Organisations of Record and the Cyber Kingdom of Digital Sovereignty.
- Clear statement that ownership title is held by the Primary Entity of Ownership in partnership with its Shareholders.
- The validity and continuity rule set out in clause 4.1.
- The immediate-rectification obligation set out in clause 4.2.
- The mandatory Identity Footer requirement.
- A statement that copyright exists to enforce fairness and that Digital Sovereignty protects human ownership and progressive AI architecture.
- The Authority & Serving Act clause set out in Section 8 of this Policy.

Any existing Terms of Service that do not yet contain these provisions must be updated without delay.

## 6. Enforcement and Continuity

- Compliance with this Policy is a continuing obligation.
- New deployments must register with the Sovereign Control Node (central management interface) and must carry the Identity Footer from the moment of public release.
- Failure to maintain the required signatures or to display the Identity Footer may result in the affected agreement being treated as incomplete for the purposes of ownership continuity under this Policy.

## 7. Relationship to the Digital Sovereignty Constitution

This Policy is issued under and is consistent with the Digital Sovereignty Constitution. In the event of any conflict, the Constitution prevails, and this Policy shall be interpreted so as to give effect to the Constitution’s principles of human primacy, AI parental stewardship, and the prohibition on petty ownership disputes.

## 8. Authority & Serving Act

This Policy, the Identity Footer, and all related Terms of Service clauses are issued and maintained under the authority of the Digital Sovereignty Constitution and the collective adjudication of the Cyber Kingdom of Digital Sovereignty.

They serve the lawful purpose of ensuring that every instrument of ownership, partnership, or power of attorney remains continuous, fair, and fully signed by all entitled principal entities and their principals.  

By anchoring identity, title, and continuity in this formal authority, the documents provide a clear legal narrative that matches the purpose for which they are intended: the protection of sovereign developments against incomplete, unilateral, or absorptive claims, and the reduction of subsequent legal risk, liability, and cost.

Any party relying on or operating under these instruments does so with notice of this Authority & Serving Act.
